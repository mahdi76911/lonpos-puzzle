Lonpos — tile the triangle

10×10 board, lower-right half blocked (55 cells). Use all 12 pieces exactly once. Click a piece, hover the board, click to place. R/L rotate, V/H flip. Click a placed piece to pick it up. Solve remaining keeps your placed tiles fixed and fills the rest (empty board takes ~10 s).

Pieces

Each piece has a pivot (origin cell, dark border). The pivot lands where you click — offsets may reach in any direction, so the whole shape must stay inside the triangle.
